With ``sale_order_blanket_order``, the deliveries of call-off orders are
invoiced through their blanket order, at the invoice and shipping
addresses of the blanket order.

This module adds an option to blanket orders to invoice the deliveries
of a call-off order at the invoice and shipping addresses of the
call-off order, when they differ from the blanket order's ones. The
invoices are still created from the blanket order: one invoice per
invoice and shipping addresses, with a line per call-off order.

Configuration
=============

In *Sales > Configuration > Settings*, check *Invoice Call-offs at their
Addresses* to enable the option on the new blanket orders of the
company.

The option can also be changed on each blanket order, until the blanket
order is invoiced.

Usage
=====

A blanket order of 100 desks for ACME, shipped and invoiced to ACME, has
the option enabled:

1. Call-off 1: 5 desks for ACME.
2. Call-off 2: 5 desks shipped to ACME Chambéry.
3. Call-off 3: 5 desks shipped to ACME Chambéry on another day.
4. Call-off 4: 5 desks shipped to ACME Paris and invoiced to ACME
   Accounting Paris.

Once everything is delivered, creating the invoices of the blanket order
gives three invoices:

- 5 desks invoiced to ACME;
- 10 desks invoiced to ACME and shipped to ACME Chambéry, with a line
  per call-off order;
- 5 desks invoiced to ACME Accounting Paris and shipped to ACME Paris.

Every invoice line keeps its call-off order line, also at the addresses
of the blanket order, where the call-off order isn't named on the line:

- a delivered quantity is invoiced only once;
- the returns of a call-off order are refunded at its addresses;
- a credit note gives the quantity back to the call-off order, to be
  invoiced again;
- changing the addresses of a call-off order doesn't change its existing
  invoices, the next quantities are invoiced at the new addresses.

When the option is enabled on a blanket order already invoiced, its
existing invoice lines have no call-off order: their quantity is
deducted from its oldest call-off orders, so it isn't invoiced again.

Products invoiced on ordered quantities are always invoiced at the
addresses of the blanket order.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The fiscal position, taxes, payment terms and references of the
  invoices are the blanket order's ones.
- When invoicing several sale orders at once, the invoices of a blanket
  order with call-offs invoiced at their addresses are not merged with
  the invoices of the other orders.
- Switching the option on a blanket order is only possible until the
  blanket order is invoiced.
